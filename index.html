Privacy Policy

FlexiView

Responsive Mobile Simulator Chrome Extension

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FlexiView. We built this tool to provide frontend developers with a fast, reliable, and completely client-side responsive mobile testing environment. Because we are developers ourselves, we respect your privacy. FlexiView does not track your browsing history, does not collect personal data, and does not require an account.

2. Core Architecture & Data Privacy

FlexiView operates 100% locally within your Chrome browser. When you use the simulator, the websites you test and the data you generate (like screenshots or custom device dimensions) remain entirely on your local machine.

  • We do not proxy your web traffic through external servers.
  • We do not use analytics trackers (like Google Analytics) inside the extension.
  • We do not have access to your local storage, cookies, or the DOM of the sites you test.

3. Chrome Permissions Justification

To function properly, FlexiView requires the following Chrome Web Store permissions. Here is exactly why we need them:

declarativeNetRequest

Required to dynamically remove X-Frame-Options and Content-Security-Policy headers locally. This allows you to test external websites inside our simulator iframes. These rules are only active when the FlexiView tab is open.

storage

Required to save your preferred device configurations (e.g., active devices, zoom level, custom dimensions) locally via chrome.storage.local so they persist between sessions.

scripting & tabs

Required to inject our local event-synchronization script into the iframes. This is what allows scrolling, clicking, and typing to mirror across multiple devices with zero latency.

4. Monetization & Mellowtel

FlexiView is 100% free. To keep the project sustainable without injecting ads or paywalls, we have integrated the Mellowtel open-source library.

Mellowtel allows you to voluntarily share a small fraction of your unused internet bandwidth to help trusted AI companies route traffic. This is strictly an opt-in feature.

  • If you choose to opt-in via our onboarding modal, Mellowtel will route background traffic through your IP.
  • If you decline, you can continue using FlexiView entirely for free without any bandwidth sharing.
  • Mellowtel does NOT have access to your personal data, browsing history, or the websites you test in FlexiView.
  • You can revoke your consent and opt-out at any time from the FlexiView settings panel.
